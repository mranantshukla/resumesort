Lab 1 · Funnels & activation

Order funnel simulator

Change each step's conversion. The biggest absolute loss of sessions is highlighted, which is where an analyst would look first. Notice how a small change late in the funnel (payment success) moves orders almost as much as a big change early on.

Lab 2 · Retention & cohorts

Cohort retention simulator

Six weekly signup cohorts. Each row shows what share of that cohort ordered again in week N after signing up. Newer cohorts have fewer weeks of data, which is why the table is a triangle. A curve that flattens instead of falling to zero means users have formed a habit.

Lab 3 · Monetization & business health

Unit economics of one order

See how GOV, NOV, take rate and contribution margin relate. The waterfall shows where each rupee of an order goes. The sensitivity box tells you which lever moves monthly margin most, which is the kind of answer a "which metric should we optimize?" question rewards.
