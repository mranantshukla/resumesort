## Books (for after the interview, or to skim one chapter now)

| Book | Why | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments*, Kohavi, Tang, Xu (2020) | The standard A/B testing reference | Paid; free ch. 1 excerpt linked above; [companion site](https://experimentguide.com) |
| *OpenIntro Statistics* | Full intro stats, with exercises and solutions | 🆓 [official PDF](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/) |
| *Ace the Data Science Interview*, Nick Singh & Kevin Huo | SQL, stats, product sense, case questions with solutions (same author as DataLemur) | Paid |
| *Decode and Conquer*, Lewis Lin | Product sense / metric frameworks for PM-style questions | Paid |
| *Cracking the PM Interview*, McDowell & Bavaro | Product questions and estimation | Paid |
| *Lean Analytics*, Croll & Yoskovitz | Choosing "one metric that matters" by business stage; very relevant to "which metric should Zomato optimize" | Paid |
| *Case in Point*, Marc Cosentino | Case frameworks | Paid |

Check your **IIT Roorkee library** (MGCL) and its digital subscriptions (e.g., O'Reilly/ProQuest if available) before buying.

## Notes
- Paid tiers and free limits change often; check each site.
- Third-party summaries (Codebasics, prep sites) are useful for ideas but not for quoting numbers. Quote numbers only from the shareholder letter.
- Some casebooks circulate on Scribd/SlideShare without permission; stick to the official club links above.
