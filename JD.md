# Job Description: Product Analyst, Eternal (Gurugram)

Source image: [JD.png](JD.png)

> Turn data into insights that shape products used by millions of users

## What you need to bring to the "table"

1. Curiosity to deeply understand user behavior, business problems, and product journeys
2. Understanding of customer experience, product metrics, experimentation frameworks, and A/B testing methodologies
3. Analyze user journeys, funnels, retention, growth metrics, and feature impact to drive product and business decisions
4. Solve ambiguous business problems using structured thinking and data-driven analysis
5. High ownership of projects from problem identification to execution, automation, and impact measurement
6. Drive automation and build scalable reporting/monitoring solutions wherever possible
7. Ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams including Product, Business, Engineering, Design, and Operations
8. Proficiency in SQL, Excel, Python, or data visualization tools is a plus

## What you will learn

That you are capable of more than you ever thought possible.
